Privacy Policy

StreamLine Auto Publisher

Effective date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StreamLine Auto Publisher ("the Application") accesses, processes, and protects information when interacting with Facebook Pages through Meta's APIs.

StreamLine Auto Publisher is an internal business tool used by authorized StreamLine Auto personnel to prepare, publish, and schedule vehicle-related content on Facebook Pages managed or authorized by StreamLine Auto.

1. Purpose of the Application

The Application streamlines the preparation and publication of vehicle listings and related business content.

Authorized operators may generate and edit post text, select and order vehicle images, publish content immediately, or schedule content for a future publication time.

The Application is not intended to be a consumer-facing Facebook application. Access is limited to authorized business users.

2. Meta and Facebook API Access

The Application uses the Meta Graph API and Facebook Pages API to perform authorized Page management and publishing operations.

Depending on the permissions approved and granted through Meta, the Application may use permissions including:

  • pages_manage_posts — to create and manage posts on authorized Facebook Pages.
  • pages_read_engagement — to access Page information required for Page-related API operations.
  • pages_show_list — to identify Facebook Pages that an authorized account or system user is permitted to manage.

The Application does not intentionally access or publish content to Facebook Pages that have not been explicitly authorized for use with the Application.

3. Information We Process

The Application may process limited information required to operate its publishing functionality, including:

  • Facebook Page identifiers;
  • Facebook Page names;
  • Page access permissions and publishing capabilities;
  • post and media identifiers created through the Application;
  • publication and scheduling results returned by Meta's APIs.

The Application is not designed to collect personal Facebook profile information from members of the public.

4. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through Meta's APIs is used only to operate the authorized StreamLine Auto publishing workflow.

This may include:

  • identifying an authorized Facebook Page;
  • uploading vehicle images;
  • creating Facebook Page posts;
  • scheduling Facebook Page posts;
  • checking whether publication or scheduling succeeded;
  • retrieving identifiers or permalinks for content created through the Application.

5. Publishing Control

Content publication is initiated or scheduled by an authorized operator of StreamLine Auto Publisher.

The Application does not independently select unrelated Facebook Pages or publish content to Pages that have not been authorized.

6. Sale and Sharing of Data

StreamLine Auto does not sell Facebook or Meta Platform data.

We do not rent such data to third parties and do not use it for unrelated advertising, profiling, or data brokerage purposes.

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers when strictly necessary to host and operate the Application.

7. Data Storage and Retention

The Application is designed to retain only the information reasonably necessary to provide its publishing functionality.

Facebook access credentials are stored as protected server-side configuration and are not intentionally exposed through public client-side application code.

Temporary vehicle images and generated post information may be stored for the period required to complete the content preparation and publishing workflow.

The Application does not intentionally maintain a permanent database of Facebook user profile information.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect application access and credentials.

Sensitive access tokens and other credentials are maintained server-side and are not intended to be exposed to unauthenticated users.
