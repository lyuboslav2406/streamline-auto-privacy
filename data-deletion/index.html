Data Deletion Instructions

StreamLine Auto Publisher

StreamLine Auto Publisher is an internal tool used by authorized StreamLine Auto personnel to manage and publish vehicle-related content to authorized Facebook Pages.

Requesting Data Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your interaction with StreamLine Auto Publisher, you may send a data deletion request by email.

Email: streamlineauto95@protonmail.com

Subject:
StreamLine Auto Publisher – Data Deletion Request

Information to Include

To help us identify the relevant information, please include enough detail to identify your request, where applicable.

Please do not send passwords or Facebook access tokens.

What Happens Next

  1. We will review the deletion request.
  2. We will identify information associated with the request that is retained by StreamLine Auto Publisher.
  3. Applicable information will be deleted unless retention is required for legitimate legal, security, or compliance purposes.
  4. We may contact the requester if additional information is required to verify or complete the request.

Facebook and Meta Data

StreamLine Auto Publisher uses Meta APIs only for authorized Facebook Page publishing functionality.

Some information may be held directly by Meta Platforms, Inc. and may therefore be governed by Meta's own data retention and deletion processes.
